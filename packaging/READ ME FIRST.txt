SlideAI (works with JSTOR Images) - Windows prototype
====================================================

No installing and no admin rights needed.

1. Before extracting: right-click the .zip > Properties > tick "Unblock" (if shown) > OK.
2. Extract the zip somewhere you can write, e.g. Documents\SlideAI-app.
3. Double-click "Start SlideAI.bat".
   (If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info" > "Run anyway".)
4. Answer the questions: a deck title, then the works you want, one per line, like
      Braque, Houses at L'Estaque, 1908
   Press Enter on an empty line when done.
5. A Chrome (or Edge) window opens on JSTOR. Log in YOUR school's way
   ("Access through your institution"). SlideAI never sees your password.
6. A contact sheet opens: pick one image per work (green = suggested), click Approve.
7. Wait while it saves your picks to your JSTOR Workspace (about 15 seconds each).
8. When asked, open JSTOR > Workspace > your folder. The export starts by itself.
9. Your deck appears in Documents\SlideAI\<your title>\works.pptx and the folder opens.

If anything stops it, double-click "Start SlideAI.bat" again and choose "continue".

Use it for your own coursework and teaching under your school's JSTOR terms.
At most 30 images per deck, at a normal human pace. Not affiliated with JSTOR or ITHAKA.
